## The entry

> One star. He comes every day, teases me with the gate, and leaves without ever once throwing the ball.

A one-line review in the dog's own voice: a loyal guard dog who has watched
the same mailman arrive every single day, rattle the gate, and never once
play fetch. The grievance is not the visit — it is the unthrown ball.
